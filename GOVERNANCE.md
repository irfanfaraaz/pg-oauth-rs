# Governance

pg-oauth-rs is maintained by [@irfanfaraaz](https://github.com/irfanfaraaz).

## Decisions

- The maintainer makes the final call on design, scope and releases.
- Larger changes are discussed in an issue or discussion first, in public.
- Security fixes may be prepared in private, then disclosed (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors who show good judgement, especially on security, may be invited to become
maintainers. This file will list them.

## Code of Conduct

Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The maintainer enforces it.
